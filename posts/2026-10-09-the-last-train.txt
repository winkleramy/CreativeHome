---
title: The last train
category: Story
excerpt: A tiny story about a station, a suitcase, and a new beginning.
---
At the station, Mara bought a ticket to the first place whose name she could not pronounce.

“Return?” the clerk asked.

She looked down at the suitcase. It held a sweater, three notebooks, and a photograph of the sea.

“Not yet,” she said.

Outside, the rain stopped. For a moment the platform shone like a river.
