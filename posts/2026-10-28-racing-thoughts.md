---
title: Racing Thoughts
category: Poem
excerpt: Incessant chatter in the dark
---

I’m hiding from my thoughts,  
Scattered by the dawn.  
I far outpace them in the morning,  
and forget my nocturnal predators aren’t gone.  

I dart from ping to ping -  
a text message here, a notification there, a visitor at the door.  
But this trail of superficial moments  
only waits for night to fall once more.  

If I listen, can I recall them?  
The incessant chatter in the dark?  
What powers the swirl of sediment,  
never settling in its jar.
