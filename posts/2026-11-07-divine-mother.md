---
title: Divine Mother
category: Poem
excerpt: Restless wanderlust the heart She bestows
---

Trap door released,  
Don't look down...  
Freedom in the sound  
of wind and air and gravity defied,  
I'm terrified.

Looping time rewind  
the future back to the past.  
How vast the universe that traps us here.  
Embrace the fear, Divine Mother Dear.  

Carrying her cubs who cry out Her name,  
and wonder at this game She plays with us.  
Restless wanderlust the heart She bestows,  
Blinded in the snowstorm of desire,  
We play with fire.
