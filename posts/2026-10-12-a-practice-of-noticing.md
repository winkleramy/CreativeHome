title: A practice of noticing
category: Essay
excerpt: On finding something extraordinary in an ordinary walk.
---
I used to think that inspiration announced itself. Lately, I suspect it is quieter than that.

This morning it was a leaf caught in the spokes of a bicycle, turning slowly in the wind. I stood there longer than I meant to.

## Begin with one small thing

A creative life can begin with a question: **what did I almost miss today?**

- The color of a shadow.
- A sentence overheard at the grocery store.
- The familiar street, changed by rain.

> Attention is a way of making room.

There is no need to turn every observation into something. Sometimes noticing is enough.
