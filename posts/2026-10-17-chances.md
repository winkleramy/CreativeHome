title: 50% Chance
category: Moments
excerpt: Sitting out under the stars, coffee in hand, a full French press on standby
---
Sitting out under the stars, coffee in hand, a full French press on standby. The air is comfortable, rated for short sleeves. No wind but maybe a slight breeze if I concentrate. 

I count two satellites passing overhead, maybe 30 degrees between their trajectories. It used to be a novelty to spot satellites, and now I can't sit out for 10 minutes without seeing one. A bright light - UFO-level bright - approaching from the south... its flashing red wing-tip lights come into view and I accept that it's only a plane flying overhead. This is thankfully unusual, so I decide I'm not annoyed by it, even though it passes directly overhead.

Amazing that I am only now noticing how much I prefer stargazing in the morning. The autumn equinox is passed, so I feel tired earlier, my melatonin tracking the sunset, and I wake up earlier, reveling in the hours of darkness lingering for me.

It's Saturday, but the demolition crew could show up. I'm told that they are working Mondays through Saturdays for a while, trying to beat the winter rains. After months of collecting data, though, I now understand that these statements from my contractor are possibilities, not absolute facts. I give today's work crew a 50% chance.
