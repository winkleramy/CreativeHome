---
title: Foundations
category: Moments
excerpt: When these landmines explode for the last time
---

I imagine the day I won't carry these familiar worries. These worries, that dot the landscape of my mind like landmines in a forgotten battlefield, explode and reset, a border of vague malaise outlining their burial ground. 

But one day they will explode for the last time. The softspots in the earth will grow roots and form a foundation. The security of my home in this place will mirror the inner refuge of my thoughts. 

Built on transformed karma, the expanse of my imagination will have a safe harbor to launch from, to return to, to carry with me always. 

Recovery will reside as family, as refuge, no longer a foreign agent to do battle against, to fight off like an incursion. Perhaps it will line my clothes like fleece. 

The walls built here will push outward rather than close in, envelop great swaths of land, of ocean, of life. The peace here will infect the neighborhood, running like wildfire up a dry mountain, transforming the earth. The freedom here will power an earthquake, shaking loose the grip of cynicism on our hearts.
