---
title: Excise this heart
category: Poem
excerpt: Writing to excise this heart
---

I'm writing to excise this heart  
and see it on the page  

I am locked in with myself  
my senses unable to take in anything else  

Besides the weight in my chest  

What do you want?  
But it doesn't know words  

I offer it some  
But they are rebuffed

Do you refuse to speak or are words not enough?
