---
title: Veil
category: Poem
excerpt: Must we all begin again?
---

I recall the veil before my eyes  
That blurs my sense of experience  
A separate presence.  
Beyond the world's cries, something dies.

I become unreachable, others drift out of reach  
A raft adrift in a tide  
Slowly separated by its persistent pull  
As inevitable as time.

What keeps me away so long?  
What brings me back each time?  
What pulls me away again?  
Beyond fear, beyond concern, beyond and beyond and beyond...

The mechanics of living fade like photographs  
Distant memories, the present the inevitable past,  
A future dissolved like salt in the ocean vast,  
Unremarkable as nothingness.

Depths and depths of darkness, cool and soothing,  
impenetrable to light.  
A refuge where dampened coals no longer feed the fire  
A cold hearth long deserted of desire.

Crumbled stone and weathered vest belies the bones beneath my chest, sun long set beyond the west of this empty nest. I gave my best but failed the test. Keep your quests for future guests! No less the bitterness of tears long past leave trails of festered blood and pus and pestilence, blest by the rusted cross of the High Priestess. Now I rest.

Dare my sorrows rise again?  
Face the wind,  
embrace my sin?  
Must we all begin again?
