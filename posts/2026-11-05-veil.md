---
title: Veil
category: Poem
excerpt: Must we all begin again?
---

I can recall the veil  
That falls before my eyes  
Before my experience  
Robs my essence  
Absent presence  
The world cries, but its wail  
Are no longer my tale  
Drowned out in a gale  
That powers the sail  
Of my raft adrift at sea  
Misery the tide that pulls incessantly  
Inevitable as time  
The separation winds down this rhyme  
The rhythm beat beneath my feet  
Is as mired as my lost desire  
A cold hearth long bereft of fire  
Cannot stoke my body on the pyre.  
Dampened coals these souls like crumbled stone  
A weathered vest belies the bones beneath my chest,  
The emptiness.
The liviing fade like photographs  
Distant memories, the present the inevitable past,  
A future dissolved like salt in an ocean vast,  
Unremarkable as nothingness.  
I gave my best but failed the test  
Blood and bone and pestilence  
No less the bitterness of tears long past.  
Rusted crossed blessed,  
Let me rest, transform and mourn,  
Pull back the veil that hides the hell,  
Quell with ocean swell, upheaving the blackness still.  
Dare my sorrows rise again?  
Face the wind,  
embrace my sin?  
Must we all begin again?
