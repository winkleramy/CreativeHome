---
title: Veil
category: Poem
excerpt: Must we forever begin again?
---

I can recall the veil  
That falls before my eyes,  
Mutes my experience,  
Depletes my essence,  
And finally marks my absence.  
The world cries and wails  
But to no avail. Beyond the pale,  
This place is just a fairytale  
Its witches whip up a gale  
On which pirates sail their fleet  
While my liferaft drifts away to sea.  
Misery is a slow tide that pulls incessantly  
Inevitable as time  
Winds down the clock, tic, then tok.  
It exhales a last poetic rhyme.  
I mark the slowing rhythm,
Its beat a dying drum sunk beneath my feet  
Mired in muck. Such is my lost desire.  
A cold hearth long bereft of fire  
Cannot stoke my body on the pyre.  
Like dampened coals, these souls crumble like loose stones.  
A weathered vest barely hides the bones beneath my chest.  
Flat emptiness.  
The living fade like photographs  
Of distant memories, and mark the present the inevitable past.  
A future dissolves like salt in an ocean vast,  
Unremarkable as nothingness.  
I gave my best and failed her test,  
This High Priestess  
Of blood and bone and pestilence.  
No less the bitterness of tears long past  
That blessed rusted crosses and banner crests.  
Let me rest at last! 

And then I must transform and mourn,  
Pull back the veil that hides this hell forelorn,  
Quell the ocean swell upheaving, weaving the blackest spell.  
Dare my sorrows rise again?  
Face the wind, accept my sin?  
Must we forever begin again?
