---
title: Excise this heart
category: Unfinished
excerpt: I'm writing to excise this heart
---

I'm writing to excise my heart  
and see it on the page  

I am locked in with myself  
my senses unable to take in anything else  
besides the weight in my chest  

What do you want?  
But it doesn't know words  

I offer it some  
But they are rebuffed

Do you refuse to speak or are words not enough?  

Before you suggest it, my hearing is fine. I note every passing car, every low hum of the refrigerator compressor, every tick of the wall clock. If my heart could whisper, I would hear it like a shout.

because feeling it blindly is too hard  
I'm getting nowhere

Locked in with myself alone for three years  
It seems I'll never leave.  

We see each other through paper walls  
and pretend we don't see or hear
